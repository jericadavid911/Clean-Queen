FAQ

Questions? We Have Answers.

Where do you deliver?＋
Across Makati, Manila, and Pasay — see the full barangay list in Delivery Coverage above. Delivery is always free. Don't see your barangay? Call or text 0921-660-6969 and we'll check for you.
Is there a minimum order?＋
Inside San Antonio Village there's no minimum — order a single gallon if that's all you need. Everywhere else, the minimum is 10 × 5-gallon bottles, which is why we're a favourite of offices and small businesses. The minimum counts 5-gallon refills only; PET bottles can ride along with your order.
How fast is delivery? Is there a cut-off?＋
Same-day delivery for orders placed before 6:00 PM. Orders after the cut-off go out the next day. We can carry up to 60 gallons per trip, so large office orders are no problem.
Do I need my own container? What does one cost?＋
Either way works. Bring your own and we'll swap it on delivery, or buy a new 5-gallon container from us for ₱200 — it's yours to keep and refill for life. We stock both round and slim containers, and every container is sanitized before refilling.
Round or slim — does it change the price?＋
No. Same price either way, and all three water types — purified, alkaline, and mineral — are available in both container shapes. Pick whichever fits your dispenser.
How do I pay?＋
Cash or GCash on delivery — whichever is easier. Our GCash number is 0921-660-6969. For office and bulk accounts, ask us about regular billing.
Is your water actually tested and safe?＋
Yes. Our purification system is FDA-approved and every batch goes through 27 stages of filtration, including reverse osmosis and UV sterilization. You're welcome to visit the station and see the setup for yourself.
How do I order online?＋
Tap Order Now, enter your email — no password or code needed — pick your water, choose a delivery date, done. First-time customers just add their name and address once.
What are your store hours?＋
We're open daily from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, including weekends. Walk-ins are always welcome at 8378 Mayapis St., San Antonio Village.
